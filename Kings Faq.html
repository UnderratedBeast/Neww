Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Welcome to the Kingsmen Footwear FAQ page! We've compiled a list of common questions to help you with your shopping experience. If you don't find the answer you're looking for, feel free to contact our customer support team.

1. What materials are used in Kingsmen Footwear shoes?

At Kingsmen Footwear, we use only the finest materials, including premium leather, suede, and high-quality textiles. Each material is carefully selected for its durability, comfort, and aesthetic appeal, ensuring that every pair of shoes meets our high standards.

2. How do I determine my shoe size?

We recommend using our detailed Size Guide to find the perfect fit. The guide includes instructions on how to measure your foot and convert measurements to our sizing chart. If you're between sizes, we suggest choosing the larger size for the best fit.

3. What is the return and exchange policy?

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your shoes, you can return or exchange them within 30 days of delivery, provided they are unworn and in their original packaging. Visit our Returns & Exchanges page for detailed instructions.

4. How can I care for my Kingsmen Footwear shoes?

Proper care will extend the life of your shoes. For leather shoes, we recommend using a high-quality leather conditioner and polish. Suede shoes should be brushed regularly with a suede brush and protected with a water-resistant spray. Check our Shoe Care Guide for more tips.

5. Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship worldwide! International shipping rates and delivery times vary depending on your location. Please refer to our Shipping Information page for more details.

6. How long will it take to receive my order?

Orders are typically processed within 2-3 business days. Delivery times depend on your location and the shipping method selected. Standard shipping within Nigeria usually takes 3-5 business days, while international shipping may take 7-14 business days.

7. Can I track my order?

Yes, once your order is shipped, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number to track your package on our website or through the carrier's tracking system.

8. Do you offer custom or bespoke shoes?

Yes, we offer custom designs and bespoke services for those looking for something truly unique. Contact our customer service team to discuss your requirements and start the customization process.

9. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept a variety of payment methods, including major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payments are securely processed to ensure your information is safe.

10. How do I join the KINGSMEN Club?

Joining the KINGSMEN Club is easy! Simply sign up on our Membership Page to enjoy exclusive benefits, including special discounts, early access to new collections, and more.

11. Can I purchase a gift card?

Yes, we offer digital gift cards that can be used for any purchase on our website. Gift cards are available in various amounts and are delivered via email, making them the perfect gift for any occasion.

12. How do I contact customer support?

You can reach our customer support team via the Contact Us page on our website. We’re available Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 5 PM (WAT). Feel free to send us an email or give us a call if you have any questions or concerns.
